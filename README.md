G20SU20 -an interactive survey app- is a central hub for organizations like startup incubators and venture studios to track how their businesses are growing. It replaces messy spreadsheets and disconnected tools with a single, automated dashboard.
---
**What it does:**

  - Combines Your Data: It pulls information from everywhere—like your CRM, spreadsheets, and social media—into one place.

  - Builds Surveys: You can create surveys that work on WhatsApp, SMS, or even offline to get feedback from founders and teams.

  - Analyzes Automatically: An AI reads through survey answers and feedback to instantly find patterns and main themes so you don't have to do it manually.

**How it works:**

  - Collect: You send out surveys through various channels (web, mobile, QR codes).

  - Organize: The platform automatically cleans and organizes this data alongside your other records.

  - Understand: The AI categorizes the feedback and predicts future trends for your startups.

  - View: You see everything on live dashboards that track key stats like funding raised, jobs created, and diversity (J.E.D.I.) metrics .

**Why it's useful:**

  - Saves Time: You no longer have to manually process data or try to make sense of hundreds of survey responses.

  - Better Decisions: It gives you a "single source of truth" with real-time updates so you always know how your programs are performing.

  - Easy to Use: It is designed to work on both computers and mobile phones, even if you are in the field with poor internet.
